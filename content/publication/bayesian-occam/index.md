publication: "*Cognitive Science*, 42(4), 1345-59"
publication_types: ["article-journal"]
abstract: "Occam's razor plays a prominent role in ordinary and scientific inference. One attractive hypothesis known as Bayesian Occam's razor (BOR) is that more complex hypotheses tend to be more flexible and that flexibility is automatically penalized by Bayesian inference. In two experiments, we provide evidence that people's intuitive probabilistic and explanatory judgments follow the prescriptions of BOR."
