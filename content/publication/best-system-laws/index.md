abstract: "In recent years, an active research program has emerged that aims to develop a Humean best-system account (BSA) of laws of nature that improves on Lewis's canonical articulation of the view. Its guiding idea is that the laws are cognitive tools tailored to the specific needs and limitations of creatures like us. While current versions of this 'pragmatic Humean' research program fare much better than Lewis's account along many dimensions, I will argue that they have trouble making sense of certain key features of the practice of fundamental physics."
