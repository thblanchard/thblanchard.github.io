publication_types: ["article-journal"]
abstract: "Intuitions play an important role in the debate on the causal status of high-level properties. For instance, Kim has claimed that his 'exclusion argument' relies on 'a perfectly intuitive understanding of the causal relation'. We report the results of three experiments examining whether laypeople really have the relevant intuitions. We find little support for Kim's view and the principles on which it relies."
